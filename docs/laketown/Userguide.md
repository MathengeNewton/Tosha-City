# LakeTown Butchery — User Guide

This guide is for **staff** using LakeTown on the web. Your administrator has already set the system up. Follow these steps in order.

---

## Before you begin

1. Use a **computer or phone** with **Google Chrome**, **Microsoft Edge**, **Firefox**, or **Safari**.
2. Make sure you are **online** (Wi‑Fi or mobile data).
3. Open the **LakeTown website address** your administrator gave you. If you do not have it, ask your manager.
4. Keep your **username** and **password** ready. If you forgot your password, use **Forgot password** on the login page or ask an administrator.

---

## Sign in

1. Go to the LakeTown login page in your browser.
2. Enter your **username** (or **email**, if your site uses it).
3. Enter your **password**.
4. Click the button to **log in** (for example **Sign in** or **Login**).
5. You should see your **dashboard** (home screen).

If login fails, check spelling, caps lock, and ask an administrator if your account is active.

---

## Check your branch (important)

LakeTown works **per branch** (each shop or counter). After you log in:

1. Look at the **top of the screen** (or the area your site uses for **branch name**).
2. Make sure the branch name matches **where you are working today**.
3. If it is **wrong**, **stop** and ask an administrator to fix your account before you record stock or sales.

---

## Cashier: your day step by step

Do these steps **in this order** every working day.

### Step 1 — Open today’s stock session

1. Open **Stock session** (or **Stock**) from the menu.
2. If no session is open for today, click **Open session** (or similar).
3. Wait until the screen shows that a session is **open** for your branch.

Do **not** start selling until the session is open.

### Step 2 — Record opening stock

1. Stay on the **stock session** screen for today.
2. Find **Opening stock** (or **Opening**).
3. For each product you count at the **start of the day**, add a line: choose the **product**, enter **quantity** and **unit**, add a **note** only if needed.
4. **Save** each line.

### Step 3 — Incoming stock (only if stock arrives)

1. If new stock is **delivered during the day**, open **Incoming stock** (or **Incoming**) inside the same session.
2. Add each delivery: **product**, **quantity**, **supplier**, **notes** if needed.
3. **Save** each entry.

### Step 4 — Production (only if you make prepared items)

If you cook or prepare items from a **recipe** (for example mutura, soup, prepared pork):

1. Open **Production** inside the stock session (or from the menu, as your site shows).
2. Click **Record production** (or similar).
3. Choose the **recipe**.
4. Enter how much you **made**, any **wastage**, and check **ingredients** match what you used.
5. **Save** the production run.

### Step 5 — Sales

1. Open **New sale** (or **Sales** → **New**).
2. Check that the **branch** at the top is correct.
3. Choose **how the customer pays** (cash, mobile money, credit, and so on — your list may differ).
4. Add **customer** only if needed.
5. Click **Add item** (or similar). Pick each **product**, enter **quantity** or amount as the screen asks, and check the **price**.
6. When all items are added, **submit** or **complete** the sale.

For **wholesale** customers, pick the customer first so prices follow your wholesale rules. For **credit** sales, pick a **credit** customer and the payment method your policy allows.

### Step 6 — Payments

If a sale is **not fully paid** when you create it:

1. Open that **sale** from the sales list.
2. Use **Record payment** (or similar) and enter what was paid and how.
3. **Save**.

### Step 7 — Wastage or stock share (only if your branch uses them)

If your manager uses **wastage** or **partner stock share**:

1. Use the menu items your site shows for **Wastage** or **Stock share**.
2. Fill in only what your **procedure** requires — ask your manager if unsure.

### Step 8 — Closing stock

1. Return to the **stock session** for today.
2. Open **Closing stock** (or **Closing**).
3. Enter **quantities** for products at **end of day**.
4. **Save**.

### Step 9 — Close the session

1. When closing stock is done and you are ready to end the day, click **Close session** (or **End day**).
2. Confirm if the system asks you.

---

## What cashiers usually cannot do

Depending on your site settings, you may **not** be able to:

- Change **another user’s** account or **branch**.
- **Delete or change** a sale that was already saved (ask an **administrator**).
- **Delete or change** a stock entry that was already saved (ask an **administrator**).
- Open **company-wide reports** or **admin** screens.

If you need a correction, tell your **administrator**.

---

## Administrator: first-time setup (order matters)

If you are setting up LakeTown **for the first time** for your business, do this **in order**:

1. **Branches** — Add each shop or outlet with the correct **type** (general butchery or pork butchery, as your business uses).
2. **Users** — Create staff accounts and assign each person to the **correct branch**. Cashiers need the right branch before they work.
3. **Products** — Add everything you sell or use, with correct **prices** and **sale mode** (weight, portion, piece, and so on).
4. **Recipes** — Add recipes for **prepared items** only after those **finished products** exist as products.
5. **Suppliers** — Add suppliers for incoming stock.
6. **Customers** — Add customers, including **credit** or **wholesale** types if you use them.
7. **Partners** — Add partners only if you use **stock sharing**.

Then check that cashiers can log in and see the correct **branch**.

---

## Administrator: daily oversight

- Use **Reports** to review sales and stock by branch and date (your screens may vary).
- Use **Activity log** if your site offers it, to see important actions.
- Use **Users** and **Branches** when staff or locations change.

---

## If something goes wrong

1. **Refresh** the page or log out and log in again.
2. Check you are on the **correct branch**.
3. Ask your **LakeTown administrator** at your company.
4. For **password** problems, use **Forgot password** or ask an administrator to reset your account.
