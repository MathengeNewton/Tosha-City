# 5. SOP And Process Flows

## Purpose
This section provides standard operating procedures that staff can follow during live work.

## SOP 1: Open A Branch For The Day

### Objective
Start the day correctly so stock and sales can be tracked.

### Steps
1. Log in
2. Confirm your branch
3. Open the `Stock Session` page
4. Open the day’s stock session
5. Confirm the session shows as open
6. Record opening stock

### Result
The branch is ready for operations.

## SOP 2: Record Opening Stock

### Objective
Capture the stock available at the start of the day.

### Steps
1. Open the current stock session
2. Open the opening stock section
3. Select the product
4. Enter the quantity
5. Confirm the unit
6. Add notes only if needed
7. Save the entry
8. Repeat for all opening items

### Result
The branch has a clean opening stock record.

## SOP 3: Record Incoming Stock

### Objective
Capture stock received during the day.

### Steps
1. Open the current stock session
2. Open the incoming stock section
3. Select the product
4. Enter the quantity
5. Confirm the supplier
6. Add notes if needed
7. Save the entry

### Result
The received stock becomes part of the session record.

## SOP 4: Record Production

### Objective
Convert ingredients into sellable prepared products.

### Steps
1. Open the current stock session
2. Open the `Production` tab
3. Click `Record production run`
4. Select the recipe
5. Enter the actual output
6. Enter wastage if there is any
7. Confirm actual quantities used for ingredients
8. Add notes if needed
9. Save the production run

### Result
The session shows what was produced, what was used, and what was wasted.

## SOP 5: Create A Standard Sale

### Objective
Record a sale correctly and move it into payment handling.

### Steps
1. Open `New Sale`
2. Confirm the branch at the top of the page
3. Select payment method
4. Select customer if needed
5. Click `Add Item`
6. Select the product
7. Enter quantity or amount using the prompt shown
8. Confirm the price
9. Repeat until all sale items are added
10. Submit the sale

### Result
The sale is created and opens in the sale details page.

## SOP 6: Create A Wholesale Sale

### Objective
Apply wholesale pricing correctly.

### Steps
1. Open `New Sale`
2. Select a customer marked as wholesale
3. Confirm the wholesale notice appears
4. Add sale items
5. Confirm the prices are correct
6. Submit the sale

### Result
The sale uses wholesale pricing.

## SOP 7: Create A Credit Sale

### Objective
Record a sale that will be paid later.

### Steps
1. Open `New Sale`
2. Set payment method to `Credit`
3. Select a customer marked for credit use
4. Add the sale items
5. Submit the sale

### Result
The sale is created as a credit sale and payment can be completed later according to policy.

## SOP 8: Record Payment

### Objective
Move an unpaid sale into paid status.

### Steps
1. Open the sale details page
2. Click `Record Payment`
3. Choose the payment method
4. Enter MPESA reference if required
5. Save the payment
6. Confirm the sale now shows as paid

### Result
The sale is paid and the receipt can be printed.

## SOP 9: Print A Receipt

### Objective
Produce a receipt for a paid sale.

### Steps
1. Open the sale details page
2. Confirm the sale is paid
3. Click `Print Receipt`
4. Allow the print window if your browser blocks popups
5. Print the receipt

### Result
The customer receives a receipt.

## SOP 10: Record Wastage

### Objective
Capture stock loss correctly.

### Steps
1. Open the current stock session
2. Open the wastage area
3. Select the product
4. Enter the wasted quantity
5. Add the reason or note
6. Add photo evidence if required
7. Save the record

### Result
The wastage is tracked and visible in the session and reports.

## SOP 11: Record Stock Share

### Objective
Track stock given to a partner.

### Steps
1. Open `Partners`
2. Switch to the `Stock Share` tab
3. Click `Record Stock Share`
4. Select the partner
5. Select the product
6. Enter quantity and unit
7. Add notes if needed
8. Save the stock share

### Result
The stock share appears in the stock share ledger.

## SOP 12: Mark A Stock Share As Paid

### Objective
Close the payment follow-up for a partner stock share.

### Steps
1. Open `Partners`
2. Open the `Stock Share` tab
3. Filter for unpaid records if needed
4. Find the share
5. Click `Mark paid`

### Result
The stock share changes from unpaid to paid.

## SOP 13: Close The Session

### Objective
Finish the working day correctly.

### Steps
1. Open the current stock session
2. Confirm sales are complete
3. Confirm required payments are handled
4. Record closing stock
5. Confirm wastage and stock share records are entered if needed
6. Click the close session action
7. Confirm the close prompt

### Result
The stock session is closed and ready for reporting review.

## SOP 14: Review Branch Performance

### Objective
Check how a branch performed over a chosen period.

### Steps
1. Open `Reports & Analytics`
2. Choose the report tab
3. Set the date range
4. Set a branch if you want one branch only
5. Review totals, tables, and branch comparison rows
6. Export Excel if needed

### Result
You get a clear view of operational performance for the selected period.

## Daily Close Checklist
Use this before leaving the branch:

1. Opening stock was recorded
2. Incoming stock was recorded
3. Production runs were recorded
4. All sales were entered
5. Payments were recorded
6. Wastage was recorded
7. Stock share was recorded if used
8. Closing stock was entered
9. Session was closed

## Escalation Rule
Stop and ask for admin support if:
- your branch is wrong
- the required product is missing
- the required recipe is missing
- the system will not allow you to close a session
- pricing looks wrong for a wholesale sale
- a production item is missing from the sale process
